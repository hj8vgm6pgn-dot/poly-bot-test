Privacy Policy

ViceOnTheIce Clips · Last updated 20 November 2026

ViceOnTheIce Clips ("the tool") is a personal tool operated by the owner of the ViceOnTheIce channel ("we"). This policy explains what data the tool handles.

Who uses the tool

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public and has no user accounts. The only social media accounts it connects to are the channel's own.

Data the tool accesses

When the owner connects the channel's accounts, the tool accesses, through each platform's official API:

  • Account information: the connected account's display name, follower count and video count.
  • Video statistics: views, likes, comments and shares for the connected account's own videos.
  • Access tokens: the tokens the platforms issue so the tool can upload videos and read the statistics above.

The tool also reads publicly available information about public videos, such as titles and view counts, to follow trends.

How the data is used

  • To upload the channel's videos to the channel's own accounts.
  • To measure how those videos perform and to track progress toward each platform's monetization requirements.

Storage and sharing

All data is stored locally on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. Video frames and transcripts of the channel's clips may be sent to an AI service provider (Anthropic) to help write titles and captions; no account information or access tokens are sent.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept for as long as the channel is active. Access can be withdrawn at any time by removing the app in the platform's settings (in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions), after which stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle data about them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
