ViceOnTheIce Clips

A personal publishing tool for the ViceOnTheIce gaming channel.

What it is

ViceOnTheIce Clips is a small tool used by the owner of the ViceOnTheIce channel to prepare short gaming videos and publish them to the channel's own accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

What it does with TikTok

  • Signs in to the channel owner's own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit.
  • Uploads finished videos to that account as drafts, using the Content Posting API. The owner reviews and posts each one in the TikTok app.
  • Reads the account's own profile and video statistics (followers, views, likes, comments, shares) to see which videos perform well.

It is not a public service. Nobody other than the channel owner signs in to it, and it does not collect data about other TikTok users.

Content

Clips are made from footage the channel has permission to use: creator clipping programs, creators who have agreed directly, and Creative Commons videos. Every clip credits the original creator.
